# Data, scores and privacy

## Prices and time

Research shows the observation dates and price basis for the selected period. For 1D, it compares the prior session’s reported close with the latest available regular-session bar. For 5D, it compares the first and last available five-minute prices across up to five trading sessions. Intraday data can be delayed. Longer ranges use adjusted closes when available. YTD uses the previous year’s final close when available. Custom ranges include both entered dates and use the first and last actual observations inside that window. A missing baseline or a single observation leaves the percentage unavailable.

The percentage is `100 × (ending price ÷ starting price − 1)`. A chart opened later in a saved tab reads current provider data. A chart saved in a chat retains its original prices and dates. Historical adjustments can change after corporate actions. Cached observations retain their dates when a provider is unavailable.

## Published analyst ratings

Stock Analysis supplies its publicly visible firm ratings, consensus, analyst count, vote distribution and low, average and high targets where published. The forecast card credits the named data provider when identified on the page. Yahoo Finance supplies a separate consensus, counts, upgrades and downgrades, and company metrics. Coverage and reporting periods differ. The app displays the publishers separately and links directly to each. A lack of publication is visible in Research. StockReplay uses the public forecast page following Nasdaq’s refusal of automated ratings requests. [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/research-hub/)

## Buy and Risk assessments

StockReplay searches public news indexes and the issuer’s SEC filings, reads supported source pages and keeps a short passage alongside its original URL when available. The interface labels headline-only records, distinguishes indexing from publication and reports which sources responded. The assistant can show an attributed passage even when its model is unavailable.

The selected model considers available observations for the stock, its sector ETF, the S&P 500 proxy, volatility, Treasury yields and dated company, sector and market news. Company inputs can include historical annualized volatility, maximum drawdown, beta, published ISS governance risk, earnings estimates and analyst targets. News carries the publisher’s title, available excerpt, publication time and original link. Recent news uses a rolling 30-day window. Assessments select dated observations across each available category. If the API rejects a request for its size, the app retries with fewer observations and shorter excerpts. The original source URLs remain attached to saved citations. [Federal Reserve news](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents.htm)

Historical volatility uses the sample standard deviation of daily log returns, annualized by the square root of 252. Maximum drawdown measures the largest peak-to-subsequent-trough decline in the observed window. ISS governance risk is on the publisher’s scale of 1 (low) to 10 (high). Analyst targets use the publisher’s reported figures.

The selected Ollama or OpenAI model independently assigns two scores from 0 to 100. Buy score measures the strength of the investment case. Risk score measures uncertainty and potential downside. They are qualitative judgments over six to twelve months. Both can be high. The saved breakdown records each factor’s effect on the buy score and on the risk score, with older observations and coverage gaps identified. Each supporting point cites a source supplied in the request. The service checks score ranges and source references before saving the answer. Assessments retain their original dated evidence when the feeds change. [OpenAI Responses API](https://platform.openai.com/docs/api-reference/responses)

## Forecasts and calendar events

Past shows observed prices. Future starts from the last available observed price and displays one prediction for each subsequent exchange session in the selected range. Its percentage compares the selected point with that observed starting price. Selecting a date on the chart reveals the price prediction for that date. Observed closes are split-adjusted and exclude reinvested dividends. For incomplete US daily history, StockReplay checks Yahoo’s short daily feed and can fill missing equity sessions from Stock Analysis after checking overlapping prices on the same share basis. The chart links its price sources and dates its last observation.

The historical prediction compares the stock’s recent momentum, volatility and drawdowns with earlier periods in that stock’s history. For each horizon it uses up to 12 nonoverlapping earlier return windows. Weak or scarce matches pull the result toward no change. It limits each historical move to the stock’s observed 2.5th–97.5th percentile range before rescaling for current volatility. The path holds its last estimate beyond the shorter of 252 trading sessions or a quarter of the available return history. When the source has only a short or flat trading history, the chart labels the last observed price as a reference. The calculation details include observed history coverage and the mean absolute error in a walk-forward check against the next observed close. The comparison uses the error from holding the previous close constant. The historical checks use up to 63 one-session outcomes, 12 nonoverlapping 21-session outcomes and four nonoverlapping 252-session outcomes, according to available history. Future event markers identify the source and distinguish confirmed dates from estimated dates.

Looking up a ticker starts generation when its selected provider and model have passed a generation check. Research pairs one price path with independent Buy and Risk scores and their dated sources. A strong investment case can carry high risk. The model cross-checks extreme scores against dated evidence, including observed volatility and drawdown when available. If a judgment still conflicts with its supporting evidence, the chart identifies that score as unresolved and excludes its influence on the price line. The generated prediction, starting quote, model, scores and citations are saved together. Reopening the same prediction reuses the saved result when its inputs are still current. With an unavailable connection, the historical prediction stays visible and **Generate now** allows a retry.

The Q1–Q4 cards identify the issuer’s fiscal periods. They show published EPS or revenue consensus only when its units and period match. Model expectations are labeled separately, and an unknown estimate stays unavailable. In Future, Year-end runs to the end of the current year and Max is capped at ten years.

Each event has a source, status and explanation of its relevance. Fiscal quarter boundaries can be estimated from a company’s reporting calendar. A scheduled earnings release, policy meeting, Senate committee agenda or published legal deadline has its own status and source. A hearing or markup date records scheduled consideration. The standalone Calendar places events alongside its historical simulation. In homepage Future, a cited event can also appear among the model’s scenario drivers. A source can change a scheduled date, and future legislation, filings or meetings may have no published date yet.

## Personal information

Accounts, statement values, quantities, cost basis, imports and watchlist groupings stay in the local database. Raw statement files are processed locally and discarded after parsing. Portfolio quote lookups send individual ticker symbols to the market provider. Portfolio totals, currency groupings and allocation charts are computed locally. When Research follows your portfolio, it selects an eligible holding and displays its weight within its statement currency, based on absolute reported position values. A fixed starting ticker or an explicit stock link can be used at any time. A new statement refreshes the holdings snapshot for the applicable account after review.

With local Ollama selected, generation requests stay on the Mac. If you save an Ollama web search key, StockReplay sends public search terms to Ollama and can read the returned public pages. Selecting Ollama Cloud sends model prompts to its hosted inference API. Each saved credential uses its own session or Keychain entry. When you explicitly select OpenAI, the key is used by the local backend for HTTPS requests to OpenAI. The session option keeps it in memory. Persistent storage uses macOS Keychain. The browser never receives a saved key when it requests connection status. Stock assessments send public evidence. Recognized navigation, chart-selection and preference commands can run locally. For requests that need the model, the assistant sends the conversation context, active page and ticker. Local action results such as account balances are rendered on the Mac and excluded from subsequent prompts. StockReplay requests `store: false` from OpenAI. [OpenAI API data controls](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/your-data)

## Saved conversations, articles and tabs

Conversations are stored in the same local SQLite database as your portfolio. A conversation stores the displayed messages, action receipts, navigation targets, cited sources and local results so you can reopen it later. While a save is pending, a draft stays in local browser storage until the database acknowledges the write. The selected model receives the recent conversational context and public research evidence needed for the request. Portfolio rows returned by a local action stay on the Mac. Chat price charts are saved as dated observations and can be reopened without a fresh quote.

A custom tab stores its name, ordered sections, stock selections and notes. Market widgets on a tab read the latest available data when opened. A saved article stores its original URL, publisher, timestamp and supplied excerpt. StockReplay caches verified photos from the news feed’s supported media hosts on the Mac, up to 48 MiB. The browser displays a photo only after it decodes. Reading the full article opens the original publication.
